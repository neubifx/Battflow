
# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and inclusive
environment for everyone contributing to or using Battflow.

## Expected behaviour

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Engage in respectful scientific and technical discussions.
- Respect the privacy of other contributors.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Offensive or abusive language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Other behaviour that undermines a respectful and collaborative environment.

## Reporting and enforcement

Unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to the project maintainer.

The maintainer is responsible for reviewing reports and taking appropriate
action, which may include removing inappropriate comments or restricting
participation in the project.

This code of conduct applies to all project-related interactions, including
GitHub issues, pull requests and other communication channels.
